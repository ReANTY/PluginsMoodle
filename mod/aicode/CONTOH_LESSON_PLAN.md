## 💡 Teaching Tips

### For Each Quiz:

**Before:**

1. Explain the concept (10 min lecture)
2. Show example (5 min demo)
3. Answer questions

**During:**

1. Monitor attempts in real-time
2. Watch for common errors
3. Intervene if needed (via forum)

**After:**

1. Review common mistakes
2. Show optimal solutions
